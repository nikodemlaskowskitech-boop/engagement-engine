Ask the documents

Search the demo accounts' support tickets, emails and call notes in plain words. You get the accounts that match best, with the line that matched and its source.

All companies, people and numbers in this demo are fictional and generated.

Try:

    What this searches, and what it can't do
    • It finds and ranks documents. It doesn't write an answer: read the matching line and judge.
    • It matches words, plus a short CS vocabulary (a "competitor" question also looks for a benchmark, an RFP, a trial). It doesn't understand meaning, so a question in unusual words finds less.
    • It doesn't understand dates, amounts or "not".
    • People appear as labels (Customer A, CS 1) and companies as "the customer": names were removed, and checked for, before the search ever saw the text.
    • It shows the top __TOP_K__ accounts. On a test set written by someone who never saw the search, the first result was right about 6 times in 10, and the top 5 held 3 of every 4 right accounts.
    • What you type isn't stored anywhere.
