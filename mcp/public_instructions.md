Cairnpath is a demo: every company, person and number in it is fictional and generated. These tools read the demo's support tickets, emails and call notes (people's names removed: Customer A, CS 1) and the demo accounts' names, tier, ARR and open flags. Documents are grouped into cases (one problem, however many messages). Search matches words, not meaning. Cite a document by its account and ID (acc_012 E1). Numbers like churn risk come from the engine and are shown as flags; the documents hold what customers and CS wrote.
