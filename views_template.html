    Did a churn call help? (Act accounts, Scale and Enterprise)

    ARR at risk vs. ARR really lost at renewal

    At risk: the data's rule ([money.at_risk] in settings.toml: the whole ARR at Act, the unused part at Watch and Shelfware). Lost: ARR minus what the account renewed at. Under 100% the rule over-calls the risk, or CS saved some of it; over 100% it's too relaxed. The renewal values are generated (a downsize shrinks to about what the account uses), so this shows the method, not proof the rule is right. Owners' forecasts join this table once their accounts renew.
