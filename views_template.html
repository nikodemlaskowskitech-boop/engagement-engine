          3. Accounts in the portfolio: risk first

          Sorted by value at stake. At risk: the whole ARR at Act (they may leave), the unused part at Watch and Shelfware (customers cut their commitment to what they use). Upside: forecast usage above the contract at renewal. "≠ data" marks a forecast a quarter of the ARR away from the data's number. Value guides the work; it isn't anyone's credit. Action planned: the owner's next step with a date (set it on the account's timeline), or a QBR booked in the CRM; overdue when the date passes with no meeting. Insight: a possible theme from the account's tickets, emails or call notes (a keyword search over the redacted documents), with its source; context for the owner, it changes no status or value until someone acts on it.

          Lesson learned: ARR at risk vs. ARR really lost at renewal

          At risk: the data's rule ([money.at_risk] in settings.toml: the whole ARR at Act, the unused part at Watch and Shelfware). Lost: ARR minus what the account renewed at. Under 100% the rule over-calls the risk, or CS saved some of it; over 100% it's too relaxed. The renewal values are generated (a downsize shrinks to about what the account uses), so this shows the method, not proof the rule is right.
